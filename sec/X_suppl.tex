\section{Rationale}
\label{sec:rationale}
% 
\subsection{Masked fine-tuning ablation reproducibility}

The complete sweep is launched by
\texttt{patchsae/run\_masking\_ablation.sh}. It trains the Cartesian product of
five target datasets, seven protected fractions, and three SAE seeds (105 runs).
The script is restart-safe and writes a manifest containing the exact checkpoint,
LoRA checkpoint, fraction, and seed for every completed run. Evaluation uses
\texttt{tasks/eval\_masking\_ablation.py}; its ImageNet subset seed is fixed at
2026 and is independent of the training seeds. Aggregation checks the number of
unique seeds per cell and retains raw per-seed measurements alongside the mean
and confidence interval. Checkpoints store the protected mask, realized numbers
of protected/free units, number of optimization steps and tokens, and whether
post-update exact restoration was enabled.

We treat target accuracy and ImageNet accuracy as the two primary outcomes.
Reconstruction cosine, FVE, $L_0$, and dead-feature fraction are secondary
mechanistic outcomes. The percentage grid and primary outcomes are fixed before
examining the completed sweep; failed runs are rerun with the same seed rather
than omitted or replaced.
